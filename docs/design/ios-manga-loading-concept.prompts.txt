Book, author, source and cover artwork below are illustrative, not connected catalog data.

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a polished, highly legible, production-ready FOUR-SCREEN mobile UI design board for NodeLane. The four full frontal iPhone screens must be identical size and aligned side by side on a clean light neutral canvas, with slender unobtrusive device outlines, 9:41 status bar, Dynamic Island and home indicator. Landscape image. Small heading at top: "NodeLane"; subtitle: "漫画详情与阅读加载". Each screen should look like a real implemented app, not a wireframe. Captions below screens: "详情首次加载", "章节目录加载", "打开章节", "加载失败". Footer: "视觉概念稿 · 示例内容".

Use the existing NodeLane visual system precisely: canvas #F5F6FA; group surface white; soft fill #ECEFF4; primary ink #171A21; muted text #768094; line #E2E6EE; accent #006EFF; accent soft #E4EFFF. SF Pro / PingFang Chinese system typography, consistent medium-weight SF Symbols-style outline icons. Detail-page gutters 16 pt, cover ratio 132 by 188 pt with 10 pt radius, groups 18 pt radius, buttons 24 pt capsule radius, icon tap targets 44 pt and actual icons 24 pt. Main title 24 pt semibold, section titles 17 pt semibold, body 15 pt, auxiliary copy 14 pt, metadata 12–14 pt. Spacing only 4/8/12/16/20/24 pt. Subtle frosted glass only on navigation controls; content surfaces flat and calm. No heavy shadows, decorative colored gradients, huge empty regions, random tinted icons, loading percentages, fake progress steps, ads, ratings, or bottom root tab bar. Do not show raw URLs, diagnostics or technical API terms.

One consistent example book in all four panels: title "海岸来信", author "海野", source "示例来源 · 中文", chapter "第12话  海风的回信". Create an original restrained coastal manga cover with blue sea, a cream sky, a small seaside town and a young adult viewed from behind. No copyrighted franchise. Known title and cover remain visible throughout loading. Skeletons are low-contrast rounded neutral bars that exactly occupy the spaces of the existing detail content; no dense shimmer glare and no fake text. All loading statuses use one clear compact progress indicator and readable muted text near the content it describes. Back navigation remains available.

SCREEN 1 — First detail load, meaningful skeleton layout:
Top navigation has only a clear back chevron in a 44 pt soft glass circle on the left; no spinner floating over the navigation bar. Cover left at 132x188 pt and title "海岸来信" on the right; show known source "示例来源 · 中文". Unknown author and status are represented by two slim neutral placeholder bars rather than empty gaps. Below the cover, horizontal actions: disabled soft neutral "加载章节…" with book icon, and existing soft-blue "加入书架" with plus icon. A white 18 pt radius synopsis group with real heading "简介" and three aligned skeleton text bars; two restrained small tag placeholders beneath. A single collapsed "更多" row can be shown as a subdued non-interactive placeholder, not an expanding panel. Below, section header "章节", small spinner and label "正在加载…" at right. White chapter group with three 72 pt skeleton rows, title and subtitle bars and a circular muted action placeholder, thin separators. Layout is full and balanced, using the actual detail page geometry.

SCREEN 2 — Known overview, only chapter catalog waiting:
Same back navigation and identical cover/title placement. Now show author "作者：海野", "连载中", and "示例来源 · 中文". Same disabled read action "加载章节…" and existing "已在书架" soft-blue action with check. White synopsis group titled "简介", blue "展开" at right, two lines of short readable text: "一封寄错地址的信，让她回到久别的海边小镇。" Small genre pills "日常" "治愈". Collapsed row "更多". Chapter section "章节" with spinner and "正在加载…". Three white grouped skeleton chapter rows exactly matching screen 1. Do not display "0 章节" or a no-results illustration while fetching. Keep existing content visible; only unknown content is a placeholder.

SCREEN 3 — Opening a selected chapter, replacement for the legacy transition:
Same canvas and a clear back control, centered compact navigation title "正在打开章节". At the visual center, slightly above the midpoint, show a 96x136 pt version of the same cover with 10 pt radius; below it title "海岸来信" in 20 pt semibold and chapter "第12话  海风的回信" in 15 pt muted text. Below with 24 pt spacing, a compact white 18 pt rounded status group with 16 pt internal padding, blue 24 pt progress ring, text "正在获取本话图片…" in 17 pt and secondary "加载完成后自动进入阅读" in 14 pt. The group fits its content; no enormous modal sheet, no bottom empty drawer, no artificial percentage or cancel overlay. No second redundant back/cancel button. This is a normal pushed page that automatically proceeds into reading when ready.

SCREEN 4 — Same chapter context, recoverable load failure:
Keep exactly the same cover, book title, chapter name and navigation position as screen 3. Navigation title "打开章节". Replace its progress group with a matching white status group: neutral ink outlined exclamation icon, heading "暂时无法加载", secondary "请检查网络后重试". A single blue capsule button "重试" at least 44 pt tall beneath, same group width. Back is still available in the top navigation. No red full-screen error, no technical stack trace, no new source-switch workflow. This panel must feel like the same page changing state, not another design.

Prioritize exact simplified Chinese, aligned baselines, consistent font sizes and group edges. The screens should make the loading state obvious without hiding known book information or claiming work is complete. Produce only the clean design board.
