Use case: ui-mockup.
Create an implementation-ready, high-fidelity iOS UI design board for NodeLane, an existing Chinese comics reading app. This is a visual design proposal for its settings/account redesign, not a screenshot of an implemented app.
Composition: wide landscape, four equally sized front-facing iPhone screens side-by-side, about 390 x 844 logical points each. Complete thin dark device frames, Dynamic Islands, 9:41 status bars, home indicators. No perspective. Quiet pale gray background, short board title outside phones, short numbered Chinese captions under phones, small footer "设计示例 · 非真实账号数据". Prioritize crisp readable Simplified Chinese. Do not cram extra content; screens can show part of a naturally scrolling page.
Strict existing NodeLane component style: canvas #F5F6FA, white solid content #FFFFFF, soft gray #EBEEF4, ink #171A21, secondary #697180, fine separators #E7EAF0, accent #006EFF, pale-blue selected state. SF Pro-like Latin and PingFang-like Chinese. Root heading34 bold, navigation/sheet title22 semibold, row label17 medium, input/body16, secondary14, small badges12. Page gutter20, group padding16, icon-to-label gap12, group gap24, vertical rhythm4/8/12/16/20/24. Content group radius18, capsule/input radius24, modal top radius24. Ordinary row min56, two-line row72. Symbols24, secondary symbols20, consistent SF Symbols-like outline family, 44 minimum hit target. Small badges height22. Avatar uses a simple neutral person symbol inside a 64pt circle, no invented personal photo. All card data rows and buttons must follow the SAME components across screens.
Localized restrained iOS Liquid Glass on top circular navigation buttons, search, sheet chrome and native tab bar, fine edge highlights and slight blur. Content cards stay solid white. No overglossy glass, gradients, promotional membership banners, colorful icon tiles, oversized charts, floating unrelated buttons, nested card-in-card borders, or arbitrary new radii.
Search is ONE soft translucent 48pt capsule, search icon left, "搜索设置" placeholder, internal clear only when populated, NO submit button, NO additional outer wrapper. Use entire tappable row with trailing chevron for navigation, not little buttons inside rows. Toggles native iOS. Blue filled capsule for a primary action, pale neutral capsule secondary, red text reserved for destructive action.
Root More screen keeps the existing native iOS Liquid Glass five-tab bar: "书架" "更新" "来源" "浏览器" "更多", 更多 selected blue. Full-screen detail pages have a back chevron and NO bottom tabs. Sheets dim the full underlying screen including native tab bar, have centered handle and identical circular X at top-right.
Product invariants: More and old Settings landing are one screen; no extra "设置" navigation row. No standalone "漫画翻译" page/row. "翻译目标语言" is a direct selector; translation-result cache belongs to storage. Reader/browser on/off and original/translated controls stay in reading context and are NOT settings-page switches. No AI provider/model/channel selection, no made-up features, no password forms, no new payment checkout, no cloud-sync entitlement promises. Subscription and quota are read-only app displays with an external website management action. Account identity, quota and subscription data shown are invented examples clearly marked outside the device.
